How We Handle Listings

Scholarship Information with Source Transparency

Each scholarship listing includes its available source information and the date it was last checked. Where a provider has not stated a funding amount, deadline, or eligibility requirement, the listing indicates that information is not stated rather than presenting an unsupported value.

Official Information Sources

Every scholarship listing includes direct links to the official provider website and original source URLs.

  • Source URL on every listing
  • Official application portal links
  • Provider contact information

Scholarship Eligibility Verification

We verify eligibility criteria against official documentation. Unclear requirements are marked as 'not stated' rather than assumed.

  • Academic requirements verified
  • Nationality restrictions confirmed
  • Language requirements validated

Deadline Verification

Application deadlines are cross-referenced with official sources. Status indicators (Open, Closing Soon, Expired) are calculated automatically from actual dates.

  • Real-time status calculation
  • Opening dates tracked
  • Admin override transparency

Funding Information Validation

Funding amounts, coverage types, and benefits are verified against official announcements. Partial information is clearly indicated.

  • Tuition coverage confirmed
  • Stipend amounts verified
  • Additional benefits listed

Regular Scholarship Updates

Our editorial team reviews and updates listings regularly. Last verified dates are displayed on every scholarship page.

  • Monthly verification cycles
  • Last verified date shown
  • Community correction system
